## Trade Lifecycle
The trade lifecycle describes the stages involved in processing a financial transaction, from execution to settlement.

1. **Trade Execution:** The buyer and seller agree on a transaction.
2. **Trade Capture:** Transaction details are recorded in the relevant system.
3. **Trade Confirmation:** Transaction details are checked by the parties involved.
4. **Clearing:** Obligations are calculated and prepared for settlement, where applicable.
5. **Settlement:** Securities and funds are exchanged according to the agreed terms.
6. **Reconciliation:** Records are compared to identify and investigate discrepancies.
